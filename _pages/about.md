**Welcome!** I'm an Associate Professor in the [Department of Economics](https://economia.uniandes.edu.co/en){:style="color:#2E5C8A;text-decoration:underline" target="_blank" rel="noopener"} at Universidad de los Andes and a Research Fellow at the Institute of Labor Economics (IZA). My research centers on labor, development, and health economics, with a regional focus on Latin America. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Oxford University.
